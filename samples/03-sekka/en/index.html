Concept

Buying the whole animal means rare cuts.

We buy whole Kuroge wagyu from carefully chosen farms and butcher it ourselves. Zabuton, misuji, tomosankaku — even the rarest cuts are recommended according to the day's condition.

Two sauces: a soy-based tare, and a salt sauce brightened with sudachi citrus. Start with salt to taste the beef itself.
