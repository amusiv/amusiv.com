Hyogo-raised Kuroge wagyu sirloin and fillet, cooked exactly how you like. Kuruma prawns from the Seto Inland Sea seared until fragrant, seasonal vegetables — our chefs finish every dish right in front of you.

Every counter seat faces the sea. We can time your reservation to the sunset and prepare a message plate for special occasions.
